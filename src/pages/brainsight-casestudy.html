BrainSightAI — Case Study
UX & Product Design

Designing for complex
neuro-tech research

Workflows, support systems, and scalable visual assets for a clinical SaaS platform serving doctors and research teams.

Role UI/UX Designer
Company BrainSightAI
Domain Neuro-tech SaaS
Tools Figma, UX Research
Overview

Context

BrainSightAI is a research-driven SaaS platform built for clinicians and research teams working with brain data and cognitive insights. The platform needed to scale — both in terms of the teams using it and the complexity of workflows it supported.

My work focused on four interconnected challenges: team onboarding, proactive user support, brand consistency, and website communication clarity.

Users served

Doctors & clinicians Research teams Internal testers Stakeholders
01

Bulk invitation flow
for clinical teams

Problem

Inviting collaborators to a workspace was time-consuming. No structured flow for roles, departments, or access control — each addition required repetitive steps.

Solution

Designed a streamlined bulk invitation system with role assignment, department selection, and workspace access control — all in a single flow.

Key capabilities

Single or bulk invitations Role assignment Department selection Workspace access control

UX considerations

Clinicians aren't power users — the interface needed to stay approachable while handling complex permission structures. Minimizing steps and preventing role-assignment errors were the primary constraints.

Outcomes

Faster team member onboarding

Improved workspace collaboration

Structured access management

02

Proactive support
system

Problem

Support was entirely reactive — users contacted support only after hitting a wall. Feature discovery issues, workflow confusion, and technical misunderstandings slowed productivity.

Solution

A proactive support system embedded across the platform, guiding users in real-time based on context — before issues arise.

Research process

I analyzed issues reported by users, internal testers, and stakeholders — then mapped and categorized them to define where and how support should be delivered contextually.

System components

✦

Contextual UI assistance

Helpful prompts that appear based on where the user is in the workflow.

◈

AI-assisted help

Smart suggestions resolving common questions without user intervention.

⬡

Step-by-step guidance

Guided action flows for multi-step processes that are error-prone.

◎

Alerts & email updates

System alerts and email notifications to prevent errors and inform users.

Impact

Reduced user confusion

Improved feature discovery

Faster issue resolution

Better platform usability

03

Brand templates &
visual identity

Problem

Existing materials lacked consistency across social posts, presentations, and product communication. No reusable design template system existed.

Solution

A template system aligned with the brand's scientific and technological identity — researched against neuro-tech, research SaaS, and AI-focused visual conventions.

Deliverables

Social post templates Presentation layouts Product communication visuals Reusable design assets Design system components
04

Website redesign
& design audit

Problem

The existing website underserved the product's neuro-tech positioning. Weak section hierarchy, unclear value communication, and inconsistent visual language reduced credibility.

Solution

Comprehensive design audit in Figma covering every section — evaluating usability, hierarchy, messaging clarity, and user journey — followed by focused redesign recommendations.

Audit scope

Section-by-section usability Information hierarchy Product messaging clarity Visual consistency User journey mapping

Focus areas

Establishing a stronger product narrative, clearer SaaS value communication, improved visual hierarchy, and design aligned with neuro-tech research credibility.

Reflections

Key learnings

01

Healthcare and research platforms require an exceptional level of clarity and trust — ambiguity in UI has real consequences for clinical workflows.

02

Proactive support systems, when designed well, significantly reduce friction — reducing the need for reactive support and improving overall usability.

03

Scalable UI patterns are critical in multi-user SaaS products — every design decision needs to account for the full spectrum of user roles and team sizes.

04

Design audits before redesigns are invaluable — they prevent solving the wrong problem and ground redesign work in real evidence.
