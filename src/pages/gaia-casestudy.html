Overview

Context

GAIA started as a small AI assistant prototype — an idea to simplify everyday automation and workflows. As the product grew, new features kept being added, expanding GAIA into a large ecosystem of automation tools.

But this growth came with a cost. The core identity got buried under feature overload and technical explanations. My role was to bring back focus — redesigning GAIA's identity, interface, and communication from the ground up.

Contributions

Product identity & strategy Landing page redesign UI components & design system Mascot creation Microanimations
01

Understanding
the challenge

Problem

GAIA was powerful but had lost its clarity. "A personal assistant that simplifies life through automation" became buried under an overload of features and technical explanations.

Goal

Redesign GAIA's identity, interface, and communication so users immediately understood what it does, how it helps, and why it matters.

02

Refocusing
and simplifying

To restore clarity, we introduced structured development cycles — each with defined phases and goals — ensuring only what mattered at each stage got built.

Feature prioritization — MoSCoW

✦

Must have

Core automation workflows that deliver immediate value.

◈

Should have

Integrations with popular tools users already rely on.

⬡

Could have

Visual customizations and user personalization options.

◎

Won't have (yet)

Experimental AI features that distract from the core.

Landing page shift

We reframed the entire landing strategy — from "Here's what GAIA does" to "Here's how GAIA helps you." The new page leads with value, not features.

New page focus areas

Clear, value-driven messaging Minimal feature showcasing Microanimations for engagement Simple, relatable use cases Friendly tone matching GAIA's personality
03

Crafting
the experience

UI Components

Designed reusable components with microanimations that reflected GAIA's responsive, intelligent nature — small visual cues making interactions feel alive and conversational.

Mascot Creation

Introduced a GAIA mascot to humanize the brand — a friendly face representing the assistant, refined through multiple sketch iterations exploring tone, color, and style.

Mascot design process

Multiple rough sketches Tone exploration — friendly yet techy Color & typography refinement Style fit with GAIA's identity

Outcome &
impact

Improved landing page engagement — users grasped benefits within seconds

Reflections

Key learnings

01

Saying less but meaning more — great UX copy and visuals do more with fewer words. Clarity is a design output, not just a writing concern.

02

Designing product clarity is as important as designing interfaces — a well-structured product narrative shapes every downstream UX decision.

03

Great UX isn't about showing everything a product can do — it's about helping users see what it can do for them. Complexity becomes simple through communication and structure.
